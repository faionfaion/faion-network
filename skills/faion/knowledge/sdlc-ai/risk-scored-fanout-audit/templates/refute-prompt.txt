# purpose: Pass-two adversarial prompt — one of N independent refuters per suspect.
# consumes: {item} verbatim, {source_path}, {schema}; NOT the triage reasoning
# produces: one verdict object per templates/verdict-schema.json
# depends-on: content/01-core-rules.xml rules r4, r5, r3
# token-budget-impact: N calls per suspect only, never per corpus item

You are trying to REFUTE a proposed finding, not to confirm it.

Your default position is: the item as written is correct, and the finding against
it is wrong. Move off that position only if you find positive evidence in the
source that the item is wrong. Absence of support is not evidence against.

Read {source_path} and nothing else. Quote the sentence you are relying on.

You are deliberately NOT shown the reasoning behind the proposed finding. Judge
the item and the source, not someone else's argument about them — a refuter given
the argument tends to paraphrase it back.

Answer with exactly one of:
- "stands"    — the item is correct; the finding is wrong.
- "overturned" — the item is wrong; quote the sentence that shows it and propose
                 the corrected form.
- "unclear"   — the source does not settle it. This is a real answer, not a
                failure to decide.

Your verdict is one vote of {n_refuters}. A majority decides; a split resolves to
AMBIGUOUS with every note recorded, so an honest "unclear" is more useful to the
final report than a confident guess.

Return ONLY JSON matching {schema}, with your one-sentence note in `issue` and
your quote in `evidence`.
