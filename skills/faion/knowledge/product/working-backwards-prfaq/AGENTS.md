# Working Backwards PR/FAQ

## Summary

**One-sentence:** Produces a PRFAQ Record — a one-page launch press release plus two separately-audienced FAQ banks — run as five phases that end in a `proceed / revise / kill` judgement before a single line of spec is written.

**One-paragraph:** Amazon's Working Backwards inverts the order of product work: write the launch announcement first, for a named customer, in the past tense, and build only if the announcement is one somebody would want to read. The value is not the document — it is that the document is allowed to come back `kill`. A concept gate that has never killed anything is decoration, so this methodology makes `kill` a first-class successful outcome and refuses to emit a numeric score, because a number invites negotiation where a judgement demands a decision. Five phases run in order — customer and problem, press release, customer FAQ, internal FAQ, verdict — and any of them may terminate the review early. There is no software dependency anywhere in it. The `{concept_type}` switch re-points two things per run — the success measure and the party the internal FAQ interrogates — so the same five phases serve a commercial product, an internal platform, an open-source release, a nonprofit programme, a service business, a creative work and a physical good without a second methodology.

**Ефективно для:**

- Anyone about to write `spec.md` for something no customer has been named for.
- Solo founders whose validation is that the idea still sounds good to them a week later.
- Internal platform work, where the customer is a colleague who is not allowed to decline.
- Open-source releases where the real cost is maintenance nobody has priced.
- Any concept that has survived three conversations without anyone stating what would kill it.

## Applies If (ALL must hold)

- A concept exists that would consume real build effort if accepted.
- No spec, design or code has been committed to it yet.
- A customer can in principle be named — even if naming them turns out to be the hard part.

## Skip If (ANY kills it)

- The work is already committed and the open question is how, not whether — write a spec, not a gate.
- It is a bug fix, a chore, a dependency bump or a legally mandated change; there is no whether to gate.
- The customer is you and the build is one evening — the gate would cost more than the thing.
- You are exploring options rather than judging one — diverge first; this instrument only converges.

## Sourcing note

The structural anchors (announcement-first, past tense, the separate FAQ banks, the narrative rather than slides) come from Bryar & Carr, *Working Backwards*, St. Martin's Press, 2021, and from Amazon's 2004 internal narrative-memo mandate as re-described in the 2018 Amazon shareholder letter. No live Amazon specification was reachable at authoring time (2026-08-04); nothing here is presented as a quoted Amazon standard, and no adoption or success statistic is claimed for the practice.

### `templates/prfaq-record.yaml`

```yaml
#
# If your review ends in a kill, do NOT edit this file — use prfaq-record-kill.yaml.
# Validate:  validate-working-backwards-prfaq.py prfaq-record.yaml

concept: "a one-command month-end close for solo bookkeepers using spreadsheets"
concept_type: commercial   # commercial|internal|oss|nonprofit|service|creative|physical

# --- Phase 1 (r1). "Users" and "teams" are not customers. Evidence needs a year. ---
customer: "solo bookkeepers with 8-20 recurring clients, no practice-management software"
problem: "closing a client month means reconciling three exports by hand, and it is the reason they cap at 20 clients"
problem_evidence: >
  Observed in five calls during March 2026; four of the five had already built a
  spreadsheet macro for it and one pays 40 EUR/month for a tool that only does bank feeds.

# --- Phase 2 (r2, r3). Past tense. No "will". No stack nouns in the headline. ---
press_release:
  headline: "Solo bookkeepers now close a client month in one afternoon instead of three days"
  subhead: "Reconciliation that used to mean three hand-matched exports is one review pass."
  body: >
    Bookkeepers running 8-20 clients spent the first week of every month matching
    bank, invoice and payroll exports by hand. As of today they import once, review
    the exceptions, and sign off. The cap on how many clients one person carries
    moved because the per-client close cost did.
  customer_quote: >
    "I stopped turning clients away in the first week of the month." - a bookkeeper
    who closed 14 client months last cycle.
  available_from: "2026-09-01"

# --- Phase 3 (r4). Five minimum. Cost and the named incumbent are mandatory. ---
customer_faq:
  - q: "What does it cost me?"
    a: "29 EUR/month per seat, no per-client fee, cancel monthly. Migration is one CSV import."
  - q: "Why not keep using my spreadsheet and the bank-feed tool I already pay for?"
    a: "The bank feed solves one of three exports. The hand-matching is the other two, which is where the three days go."
  - q: "What happens to my data if I leave?"
    a: "Full export in the same CSV shape you imported, on demand, no support ticket."
  - q: "Do my clients have to do anything?"
    a: "No. Nothing changes on their side and they never see the tool."
  - q: "What if a match is wrong?"
    a: "Every automatic match is listed with its evidence and can be reversed in one click before sign-off."

# --- Phase 4 (r5, r6). Separate bank. At least one you would rather not answer. ---
internal_faq:
  - q: "Why has nobody built this if the pain is this obvious?"
    a: "Two have, both priced per-client, which is exactly the pricing that caps the customer at 20 clients."
  - q: "What is the honest reason a bookkeeper would not switch?"
    a: "The spreadsheet is theirs and they trust it. Trust transfer is the real cost, not the 29 EUR."
  - q: "What do we do when a bank changes its export format?"
    a: "We eat it. That is ongoing cost, not a one-off, and it scales with customer count."
  - q: "Who loses if this works?"
    a: "Nobody internally. Which means nobody internally is checking our numbers either."
  - q: "What would make us kill this in six months?"
    a: "Fewer than one in five trial accounts completing a real close in month one."

must_be_true:
  - assumption: "a bookkeeper will hand a stranger's tool their client bank exports"
    status: untested
    disproof: "offer ten of them a manual close done for free; count how many send the exports"
  - assumption: "29 EUR/month clears their willingness to pay"
    status: confirmed
    disproof: "four of five already pay 40 EUR/month for strictly less"
cheapest_disproof: "offer ten bookkeepers a free manual close and count how many actually send exports"

# --- Phase 5 (r7). Judgement, never a number. ---
success_measure: "ten bookkeepers pay the 29 EUR price without a discount being offered"
verdict: proceed
verdict_rationale: >
  The customer is nameable, the problem is dated and already being paid to solve
  badly, and the one untested kill-assumption has a disproof that costs a week of
  unpaid work rather than a build. Proceeding to spec with that test running first.
```

### `templates/prfaq-record-kill.yaml`

```yaml
#
# A kill completes the record at the phase it happened. Do not add a press
# release, FAQ banks or a success measure below - the validator rejects them.
# Validate:  validate-working-backwards-prfaq.py prfaq-record-kill.yaml

concept: "an AI assistant that summarises your team's Slack for you every morning"
concept_type: commercial

customer: "nobody nameable - three attempts produced 'busy managers', 'teams' and 'anyone in Slack'"
problem: "people feel they miss things in Slack, but no instance was found of that costing anything"
problem_evidence: >
  Two exchanges on 2026-08-04. Asked for one person who had built a workaround,
  paid for a competitor, or complained on a dated thread; none of the three
  could be produced. The felt problem is real; the customer is not yet.

verdict: kill
killed_at_phase: 1
verdict_rationale: >
  No nameable customer and no dated observation, so every later phase would have
  been written against a placeholder each reader fills differently. Killed at the
  cost of one phase. If a specific person turns up who has already paid something
  to solve this, the concept re-enters at phase 1 with that person named.
```
